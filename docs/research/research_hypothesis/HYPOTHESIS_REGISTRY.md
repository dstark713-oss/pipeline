## Status vocabulary
- `proposed`: drafted but not yet approved for research.
- `approved`: approved for research design; no empirical claim implied.
- `in_research`: approved experiment is being executed.
- `supported`: evidence supports the predeclared claim within stated limits.
- `rejected`: evidence fails the predeclared claim.
- `inconclusive`: evidence is insufficient or unstable.
- `deprecated`: no longer active; preserve the reason.

## Evidence and promotion rules
- `supported` is not universal proof and must include scope, periods, uncertainty, and limitations.
- Statistical association, predictive value, and economic value are separate gates.
- Direct Monetization requires net-of-cost evidence.
- Risk Control requires an economically meaningful baseline and cannot rely solely on reduced exposure.
- A hypothesis cannot be promoted because of favorable in-sample returns or an uncorrected significant test.
