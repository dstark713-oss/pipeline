## Priority criteria
1. Potential economic value in the relevant category.
2. Strength of economic mechanism.
3. Evidence and constraints supplied by EDA.
4. Availability and temporal integrity of data.
5. Ability to falsify the hypothesis with a tractable design.
6. Risk of confounding, selection bias, and multiple-testing false positives.
7. Implementation feasibility and likely net value after costs.

The order can change only with explicit reasoning and recorded evidence. Do not prioritize merely because a hypothesis is easy to calculate.
