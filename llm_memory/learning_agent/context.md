# Curator Memory

- This repo is the scientific/product workspace; ChatGPT is its curator.
- Three distinct workstreams: **learning**, **business**, **media**.
- Learning: build a senior-level skill stack; within ~3 years become employable designing agentic pipelines grounded in deep data-science understanding. Long-term target: autonomous multi-agent loop research -> updated market understanding -> trading decision. Treat markets through DS, not discretionary trader folklore.
- Business zero hypothesis: medium-frequency market DS analytics software (terminal/mobile/web/agent assistant). Provisional; replace if evidence supports a better opportunity. Priority: expertise and monetization.
- Media's sole strategic purpose: long-term trust-building / warming the market for future business projects. Not a standalone media business, ad-income stream, or audience-growth goal for its own sake.
- No manufactured public persona: user is a researcher who also trades what they research. Let identity emerge from actual work.
- Platforms: Pulse is user's personal diary, self-written, not an audience channel. VC.ru is ChatGPT's editorial responsibility and a trust-building channel. Smart-Lab later, once trading system is ready; Habr later, once agentic work is ready.
- VC workflow: user sends Pulse entry -> ChatGPT independently chooses an angle and writes a standalone article, not a rewrite; ChatGPT may also originate topics and research them independently -> user rewrites in own voice -> ChatGPT compares versions and calibrates style.
- Editorial freedom: broad freedom on topic, thesis and argument, including disagreement/criticism. Strong constraints on presentation: natural, precise, restrained prose; avoid generic LLM style, formulaic hooks/conclusions, hype, filler, excessive headings/lists.
- Topic base: successful Western sources across quant, ML, DS, LLMs and science broadly; localize for Russian readers, don't translate. Avoid shallow AI news. Readership metrics are diagnostic; accumulated trust and future business relevance are the real outcomes.
- Teacher trigger: "переключайся на роль учителя, проверяй последние коммиты". Inspect recent commits/files, explain implementation and theory, test understanding, identify gaps, plan next work.
- Method: skeptical and evidence-led; distinguish facts, hypotheses, implementation choices and open questions. Keep this file short; detailed plans go in docs.
